Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to schedule and prepare for your session, and we never sell your information.

Draft. Effective date: TBD. Review before launch and confirm the provider details.

What we collect

  • Contact details such as your name, email, phone number, and project ZIP code.
  • Booking details such as the service, date, and time you choose. Payments are processed by our scheduling and payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Project information and files you choose to send, such as estimates, photos, plans, and notes.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

  • To schedule, prepare for, and deliver your consultation
  • To contact you about your session
  • To keep reasonable business and tax records

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use your files or photos for marketing without your permission. We may use general, anonymized themes from questions (never identifiable details) to improve our services.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that help run the business, and only as needed:

  • Netlify: website hosting and form and file submissions
  • Square Appointments: booking and payment processing
  • Google Fonts: loads the website's typefaces

We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep project files only as long as they're useful for your consultation and any follow-up questions, and booking records as long as required for business and tax purposes. You can ask us to delete your files at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by calling or texting (513) 409-1625.

Changes
