Materials & craft

The right wood and the right finish for how it'll be used.

The same knowledge behind my Material Selection sessions goes into every build. I'll recommend a species and finish based on where the piece lives and how hard it works. Ted to confirm the lists below

Woods I work with

  • Black walnutDeep brown, rich grain
  • Hard maplePale, dense, the classic board wood

  • White oakStrong, with bold grain

Finishes

  • Food-safe oil and beeswax on anything that touches food.

  • A harder topcoat where a piece needs extra protection.

How do I care for a cutting board?

Hand wash with mild soap, dry it standing on edge, and never put it in the dishwasher or leave it soaking. Rub in food-safe board oil whenever it starts to look dry, about once a month with regular use. Every board comes with care instructions.

Is this separate from your consulting?

Yes. Woodworking is pieces I build in my own shop. I don't build or install anything as part of a consulting client's remodel, so my advice on your project stays independent.
