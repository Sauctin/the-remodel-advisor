Questions

Good to know before you book.

Are you a contractor? Will you try to sell me the job?

No. The Remodel Advisor is independent consulting only. I don't perform the construction work, so my only job is to help you understand your project and make the right call for your home.

Is this the same as a home inspection?

No. Sessions are advisory. They aren't a formal inspection, engineering assessment, code certification, or licensed electrical, plumbing, or HVAC work. If your project needs one of those, I'll help you understand what to ask for.

Do you meet in person or online?

Both. I can meet at your home within about 25 miles of ZIP code 45150, or by video. The service area is estimated by ZIP code, so please call or text (513) 409-1625 to confirm an at-home visit near the boundary or to arrange a video session.

What should I have ready?

Whatever you have: contractor estimates or bids, photos of the space, measurements, plans or drawings, product links, and a rough budget. You can upload them here before your session.

How does payment work?

You pay securely when you book, by credit card, debit card, or digital wallet. Prices are flat: $50 for an Estimate Review, $40 for Material Selection, and $100 for a Renovation Consultation.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Square collects full payment when you book. To reschedule or cancel, call or text (513) 409-1625 at least 24 hours before your session. Cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance receive a full refund; cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice and missed appointments are non-refundable. If I need to cancel, you can choose a full refund or reschedule.
